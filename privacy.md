# Showing Scheduler - Privacy Policy

Showing Scheduler is a private tool used by Pat Wilver to reply to rental inquiries and schedule property showings in Savannah, GA. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: the Gmail inbox where rental inquiries arrive, and the Google Calendar where showing times are kept. It reads inquiry emails, sends replies to those inquiries, and adds showing appointments to that calendar.

How data is used: only to respond to rental prospects and schedule showings. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Message text may be processed by an AI service solely to draft replies.

Retention: lead records are kept only as long as needed for leasing and are deleted on request.
